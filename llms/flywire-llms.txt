# Flywire
> Flywire is a global payments enablement and software company, combining a proprietary global payment network, powerful payment platform, and industry-specific software to securely deliver payments for clients in education, travel, healthcare and B2B.

## Core Platform & Solutions
- [The Flywire Platform](https://www.flywire.com/platform): An overview of our platform capabilities, including security and compliance, tracking and reconciliation, and around-the-clock, multilingual support to enable faster payment processing.
- [Flywire's Global Payment Network](https://www.flywire.com/platform/global-payment-network) Our robust network of global, regional, and local banking partners enables us to offer convenient payment options globally, supporting 140+ currencies in over 240 countries and territories.
- [Payment Integrations](https://www.flywire.com/platform/integrations) We offer customized integration options so clients can tailor the payment experience based on payers' needs; Flywire also integrates directly with ERP, CRM, EHR and accounting software.

## Authoritative Industry Solutions
- [Payments for Education](https://www.flywire.com/industries/education): Official information on our solutions for educational institutions and students, including tuition billing, payments and payment plans, automated collection management, third-party invoicing, and agent payment management.
- [Payments for Healthcare](https://www.flywire.com/industries/healthcare): Information on solutions for patient billing and engagement, integrated financing, and end-to-end payment services to enhance revenue cycle management.
- [Payments for Travel & Hospitality](https://www.flywire.com/industries/travel): Secure payment solutions for travel operators, hospitality businesses, destination management companies, and their clients. Hospitality-specific solutions also outlined at https://corp.sertifi.com (Flywire acquired Sertifi in 2025).
- [Payments for B2B](https://www.flywire.com/industries/b2b): Solutions for streamlining the invoice-to-cash process for B2B companies by integrating payments into accounts receivable workflows. Invoice-to-cash solution features also outlined at https://www.invoiced.com (Flywire acquired Invoiced in 2024).
